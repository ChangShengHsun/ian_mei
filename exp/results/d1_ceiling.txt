READ THE CONTROLS FIRST.
  oracle vs shuffled: if these are close, oriented dilation is
    just dilation and every verdict above is void.
  oracle vs predicted: the part of the prize lost to the direction
    HEAD rather than to the mechanism. A large gap says the next
    effort is a better head; a small one says it is the layer.

The prize is 21.8 points: 16.7 for covering the centreline the
prediction runs beside, 5.1 for the severing breaks. The other
19.9 points between the two tables above are erl.py's splitting
rule and are not available to any method.
