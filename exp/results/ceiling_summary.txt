headroom  = oracle_sever - raw: everything a perfect linker adds.
unclaimed = oracle_sever - closing_fair: what is left after blind
            closing has taken what it can AT THE ORACLE'S OWN DICE
            COST. C1's real budget.
fg        = foreground relative to raw. This column is why the
            unconstrained closing is not a valid baseline: it buys
            run length by painting more, which ERL rewards and
            Dice charges for.
oracle_all is the bound for ANY post-processing: the distance from
oracle_sever to it is tree that no linker can reach, because
nothing was predicted near it. That part is D1's and B1's problem,
not C1's.

Both oracle columns use ground truth by construction. They are
upper bounds, not methods, and nothing here is a claim about a
model. The one decision they support is where the next weeks go.
