  `edges` is the field's convention (the Allen implementation sums
  physical edge lengths). `pixels` is what erl.py has always used;
  it undercounts diagonal steps, but numerator and denominator
  take the same correction, so the FRACTION barely moves.

  `diameter` asks a different question: not how much wire is
  connected, but how far ONE traversal gets. A tree cannot be
  covered by one path, so it is reported against its own ceiling.
  Where diameter falls much further than edges, the prediction is
  keeping the tree connected but not keeping any long path clean.
