Reading it: if gap 1 holds at every capacity, the input side
matters independently of capacity. If it shrinks toward zero,
augmentation was partly standing in for a model too small. Gap 2
is E13's original question: whether the loss starts to matter once
there is capacity for it to matter.

The two protocols differ only in WHICH epoch's weights were
scored, from the same trainings. A gap that holds under one and
not the other is a gap that depends on how long the losing arm was
allowed to overfit, which is a fact about the schedule, not the
method. best.pt is the best VALIDATED epoch, and validation runs
every 10 epochs -- a true optimum
between two validations is not visible to either protocol.
